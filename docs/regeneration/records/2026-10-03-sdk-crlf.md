## Required acceptance semantics (for the reviewer's repair decision)

CRLF is an alternate line ending at the same frame boundary: a CRLF body must
parse identically to its LF twin, across arbitrary byte/chunk splits
(including `\r` | `\n`), preserving all existing LF behavior, event types,
malformed-line/comment skipping, callback timing, `done`-before-`memory_update`
and the `event: shutdown` abort. The likely minimal repair is buffer-level CRLF
normalization in `parseSSEStream` — **the reviewer must inspect `sdk/src/sse.ts`
and decide; nothing was fixed here.** After any authorized repair, the frozen
v1.1 gate must pass 30/30 exit 0 with the baseline 25 unchanged, and
`sdk/README.md` may then be updated only under the later-write permission.
